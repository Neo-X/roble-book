Many real deployments involve multiple agents — whether robots collaborating on a task,
competing for resources, or interacting with people. This lecture covers the additional
challenges that arise when the environment itself contains other learning agents.

### Key topics

- MARL settings: cooperative (QMIX, MAPPO), competitive, and mixed
- Non-stationarity in multi-agent learning
- Human-robot interaction: modeling human intent and preferences
- Safe operation alongside humans
