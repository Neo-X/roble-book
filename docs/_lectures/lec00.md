This lecture frames the central question of the course: why can't we simply program robots to do what we want?
Classical control works well when the dynamics are known and the environment is structured, but breaks down at scale.
Learning-based methods offer a path toward robots that can acquire skills from data and generalize across situations.

### Key topics

- The gap between classical robotics and modern robot learning
- Three paradigms: imitation learning (BC), reinforcement learning, and model-based RL
- Why deep learning changed what is possible for robot perception and control
- A tour of recent milestones: locomotion, manipulation, and foundation models for robotics
