A robot that only works in exactly the conditions it was trained on is of limited use.
This lecture covers the spectrum of generalization — from multi-task policies to meta-learning
to leveraging internet-scale pretrained models for zero-shot task transfer.

### Key topics

- Multi-task learning and shared representations
- Meta-learning: MAML, RL² and fast adaptation
- Pretrained visual representations for robotics (R3M, MVP, DINO)
- Foundation models as generalist robot policies (RT-2, Octo, π0)
- Connections to sim-to-real (Lecture 18) and goal conditioning (Lecture 11)
