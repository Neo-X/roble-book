This lecture surveys the landscape of value-based and hybrid methods, bridging
actor-critic approaches (Lecture 07) with Q-learning (Lecture 08) and introducing
more recent developments like Soft Actor-Critic and distributional critics.

### Key topics

- Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) and entropy regularization
- Distributional RL: learning the return distribution, not just its expectation
- Off-policy learning and importance sampling
- Unifying policy gradient and value-based perspectives
