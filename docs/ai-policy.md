## Rules for Generative AI Usage (ChatGPT and similar tools)

It is okay to use ChatGPT and similar tools in this class.

However, simply pasting a question into these systems and copying the answer to your homework is considered [ghostwriting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghostwriter) and will not be tolerated — it will be treated as cheating. Think of ChatGPT as a friend who can provide hints to certain questions; just copying their work is still cheating. In this class, students get credit for showing what they learned.

---

### Usage Guidelines

The use of generative AI (e.g. ChatGPT) is **generally permitted** in this class, with the following requirements:

1. **Prohibited for conceptual questions and paper summaries.** The point is for students to think about and engage with the question themselves.

2. **Verify generated content.** You must describe how you verified that the AI-generated content is correct.

3. **Include a full AI appendix.** Add a detailed appendix to your submission listing all your prompts to the AI and the exact responses received.

4. **Cite AI use in your work.** In comments in your code or as footnotes in your reports, reference the relevant prompt from your appendix.

5. **Include an AI usage statement.** Write a brief section explaining, quantitatively and qualitatively:
   - How you used generative AI
   - Risks to the truthfulness, accuracy, and quality of your work
   - The concrete steps you took to mitigate those risks
   - The value you yourself added to the AI-generated content (e.g., corrections, steering)
