### Relevant Textbooks

Copies are [reserved in the UdeM library](https://umontreal.on.worldcat.org/courseReserves/course/id/18338148).

- [Deep Learning](http://www.deeplearningbook.org/) — Goodfellow, Bengio & Courville
- [Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (2nd ed.)](http://incompleteideas.net/book/the-book-2nd.html) — Sutton & Barto
- [Algorithms for Reinforcement Learning](http://www.ualberta.ca/~szepesva/RLBook.html) — Szepesvári
- [Dynamic Programming and Optimal Control, Vols I & II](http://www.athenasc.com/dpbook.html) — Bertsekas
- [Markov Decision Processes](http://www.wiley.com/WileyCDA/WileyTitle/productCd-0471727822.html) — Puterman
- [Approximate Dynamic Programming](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/book/10.1002/9781118029176) — Powell
- [An Introduction to Deep Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.12560.pdf)
- [Foundations of Deep Reinforcement Learning: Theory and Practice in Python](https://www.amazon.com/Deep-Reinforcement-Learning-Python-Hands/dp/0135172381/) — Graesser & Keng *(good introductory coverage up to DQN)*
