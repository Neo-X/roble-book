### Piazza / Discord

> **Important:** We will encourage student participation on Piazza/Discord rather than answering right away — we will wait until other students step up and answer questions. We will still provide clarifications on logistics, typos, and subtle points. We want to make sure you are helping each other out.

Follow these guidelines when posting questions — questions that follow them are much more likely to get answered.

**1. Ask homework questions only in homework posts.**
We create individual posts for each problem. Before asking, read through (or search) the whole post to see if your question has already been answered.

**2. Don't post answers.**
You can explain things in a way that lets others figure out the essence of the problem on their own, but don't spoil it. If unsure, post privately to instructors first.

**3. Try to make posts public.**
Others likely have the same question. Public posts avoid duplicate answers.

**4. Piazza/Discord is not Office Hours — apply the 5-minute test.**
If your question might take more than 5 minutes to answer, come to office hours instead.

**5. Neither Piazza/Discord nor TAs are for pre-grading.**
Don't ask "Is this the correct solution?" or "Would this receive full credit?" Come to office hours for that kind of feedback.

**6. Post a screenshot of any resource you reference.**
Your question should be self-contained. Don't make TAs scan through PDFs to understand your question.

**7. Post all your work.**
Don't post a single line like "is X = Y?" Provide enough detail to get useful feedback.

**8. Re-grading**
If you believe a grading mistake was made, submit a regrade request within one week of grades being released. Outline where the mistake occurred and why your answer is correct. Note: when a regrade is requested we review the entire question, so it's possible other issues may surface. Only request a regrade if you're confident there is a mistake.
